Our story

Raised by hand, one run of creek at a time.

Delaware Run Farm has been in the family since 2018 — what started as two ponies and a handful of hens has grown into a working barn, a small Cavalier breeding program, a farm stand, and now a place for visitors to stay and see it all up close. The name comes from the little creek that runs the length of the property — Delaware Run — which the horses drink from every day.

— The Family at Delaware Run

Farm manners

A few things that keep everyone — people and animals — safe.

Ask before you pet

Ponies are friendly, but they're athletes. We'll introduce you.

No outside treats

Every animal here is on a careful diet. Ask us for a pony-safe snack.

Close every gate

Behind you, every time — even if it was open when you found it.

Boots, not sandals

It's a working farm. Closed-toe shoes for anyone visiting the barn.
