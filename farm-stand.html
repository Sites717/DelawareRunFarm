Every day of the year

The eggs — and the honey — that started it all.

Our flock free-ranges across the pastures between the paddocks, and our hives sit along the tree line at the edge of the ag fields. Eggs are collected daily; honey is harvested twice a year, late June and again in September.

Free-range, pastured hens ~40 hens Raw, unfiltered honey

Through the year

Eggs and honey all year. Everything else, when it's ready.

Spring · April–June

Eggs daily, plus the first pressing of honey from the spring nectar flow and early greens — lettuces, spinach, and asparagus as it comes in.

Summer · July–August

Eggs daily, plus tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, summer squash and whatever else the garden is giving us that week.

Fall · September–November

Eggs daily, plus pumpkins, winter squash, apples and the fall honey harvest. Our busiest stretch of the year.

Winter · December–March

Eggs daily, plus honey and our own pickled preserves, while the fields rest under snow.
