Every day of the year

The eggs — and the honey — that started it all.

Our flock free-ranges across the pastures between the paddocks, and our hives sit along the tree line at the edge of the ag fields. Eggs are collected daily; honey is harvested twice a year, late June and again in September.

Free-range, pastured hens ~40 hens Raw, unfiltered honey

Through the year

Eggs and honey all year. Everything else, when it's ready.

Spring · April–June

Asparagus, rhubarb, early lettuces and spinach, strawberries toward June. First pressing of honey from the spring nectar flow.

Summer · July–August

Tomatoes, sweet corn, peppers, cucumbers, summer squash, blueberries and blackberries — pick your own by arrangement.

Fall · September–November

Winter squash, pumpkins, apples, late tomatoes, and the fall honey harvest. Our busiest stretch of the year.

Winter · December–March

Eggs and honey continue, plus stored squash, root vegetables and our own canned preserves while the fields rest.
