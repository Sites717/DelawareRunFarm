The four Cavalier colors

Which one is yours?

Blenheim

Rich chestnut markings on a pearly white coat — the classic Cavalier look.

Tricolor

Black and white with tan markings over the eyes, cheeks, and legs.

Black & Tan

Jet black with tan points — striking and a little less common.

Ruby

A solid, rich red all over — no white at all.

Join the litter list

We place pups in the order families join.

A $300 deposit holds your place once a litter is born. Tell us a little about your home below.

Can we visit before choosing?

Yes — once the litter is at least 4 weeks old, families on the list are welcome to visit by appointment before picking their pup.

Do you ship puppies?

We don't ship cargo. We'll help coordinate an in-cabin flight nanny or meet within a few hours' drive of Watsontown.

Is the deposit refundable?

Deposits are transferable to a future litter but non-refundable once a litter is born, since it holds your place over other families.
